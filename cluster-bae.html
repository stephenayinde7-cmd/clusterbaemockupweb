About Cluster Bae

Precision lashes, applied with care

Cluster Bae is a Gbagada-based lash studio trusted by thousands online for precise, natural-looking cluster sets and hybrid lash mapping. Every set is customised to your eye shape, whether you wear your lashes soft and everyday or full glam.

Beyond appointments, Cluster Bae also trains lash techs and shares cluster application know-how — clean tools and an unhurried appointment, every time.
